## Data availability limitation

No team raw CSV datasets are present in this branch at the time of this report.
Accordingly, this document intentionally contains no production findings,
rankings, acquisition recommendations, or numerical claims. Analytics should be
run only after Person 1 supplies the validated raw datasets.

## Evidence-based insight template

Use the following structure after executing the analytics layer against the
validated synthetic dataset. Each statement must describe an observed historical
association, not causal impact.

### Finding

State the measured pattern, scoped to the dataset and period analysed.

### Evidence

Provide the population, metric definition, comparison, and supporting values.

### Interpretation

Describe the historical relationship using language such as "associated with" or
"correlated with". Do not claim causation.

### Business implication

Offer a testable decision or monitoring action, clearly marked as a hypothesis
for future validation rather than an assured outcome.
